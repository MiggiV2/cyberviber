› cyberviber

Claude Code session manager for the 2.4" ESP32 touch display. Five layout directions, each rendered at the real 240×320 resolution. Served by the daemon (cyberviber sim) it shows your live sessions and tapping one asks the daemon to jump to its terminal; opened as a file it runs on demo data.

!needs you — permission prompt or question ✓ready — turn finished, your move ✻working — thinking / running tools ◦idle — untouched for a while

Sort order everywhere: needs you → ready → working → idle, then most recent first. The thing you should look at is always top-left.

Colour logic: state colours are the theme's own red / yellow / green, so they stay readable in every Omarchy palette. The theme accent is only used for chrome: focus, selection, toasts.

LVGL feasibility: everything here is flat rectangles, 1–2px borders, solid fills and one monospace font. No blur, gradients or shadows on the device. Blinking is a timer toggling a style. Tiles use lv_obj with flex/grid layout; lists map to lv_list-style containers.

Fonts: JetBrains Mono here; on the device, convert it (or CaskaydiaMono, Omarchy's default) with lv_font_conv at 10/12/14/22px. Include the glyphs ✻✢✳✶✽✓◦›⟶ for the spinner and icons.

Touch targets: the panel is resistive/capacitive at ~166 ppi. Rows under ~28px (option C) will be fiddly. B, D and E are the most finger-friendly.
